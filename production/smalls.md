# The Captain’s Hat — Robert Smalls

A character-centered reconstruction of the 1862 Planter escape, followed by Smalls’s later congressional service. The crew and family carry the emotional stakes.

- Picture: 60 seconds, 1,800 frames, 196 caption words.
- Actions include conferring, walking, boarding, steering, adopting a disguise, signaling, greeting and reuniting.
- Smalls imitates the captain while another freedom seeker steers; the family remains concealed during the forts passage.
- Reviews fixed cropped faces, a structural outline crossing the child’s face, flag visibility, period caps and a distracting foreground prop.
- Narration pauses were shortened outside word boundaries; pitch-preserving time compression brought speech to 59.6 seconds.
- Footsteps match the walk contacts. Generic steam effects do not claim to reproduce the historical signal code.
