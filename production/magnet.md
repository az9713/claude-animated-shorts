# The Magnet That Fell in Slow Motion

An apparent contradiction opens the story: copper can brake a falling magnet even though it does not attract it like iron. An ordinary cylinder, a strong magnet and a slit-tube comparison structure the explanation.

- Picture: 60 seconds, 1,800 frames, 210 caption words.
- Daylight demonstration moves into blue X-ray illustrations and a warm comparison.
- Reviews corrected magnet visibility, tube clearance, force direction, residual-current paths beside the slit and closing framing.
- Fields, speeds and heat particles are qualitative illustrations, not measured or numerical results.
- The next episode shifted attention toward characters in response to feedback that this short focused too much on the magnet.
